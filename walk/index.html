What holds this up

Native in MapLibre today

  • one floor per feature at its own height — fill-extrusion-base / -height, data driven
  • changing floor without restyling — global-state read from every filter
  • labels, doors and lifts at floor height — symbol-height-offset: absolute (v6.6.0)
  • eye placed at 1.7 m looking ahead — calculateCameraOptionsFromTo
  • knowing what you clicked — queryRenderedFeatures

Written in this page, and that is where it belongs

  • walls and columns stopping you, floors you cannot fall off
  • routing between two points, across floors, through stairs and lifts
  • turning OSM level=-4;-3;-2 into numeric min and max (the conclusion of style-spec#1843)

Still missing, measured here

  • the camera stops at the datum. With no terrain it cannot go below 0, so the station is lifted 30 m to be walkable. Switch datum to real to see the pitch drift on its own.
  • underground geometry needs an unmerged PR. Negative base is in the spec (style-spec#1794) but the renderer PR (gl-js#8069) is open.
  • no way to cut the roof open from outside, so you can only look in from above. style-spec#1661.
  • wall shading turns off as soon as a floor has a base, so every stacked floor is flat. gl-js#6482.

Nothing here needs a new style spec property except the reveal blend. The rest is renderer and camera correctness, plus writing the level convention down.
